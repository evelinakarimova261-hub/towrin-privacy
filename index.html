TOWRIN — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game TOWRIN (package name com.admin.tower, "the Game") handles information. The Game is developed by Elbek ("we", "us"). By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

If you sign in with Google Play Games Services, Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID, display name and avatar. This is used only to enable sign-in, achievements, leaderboards and saved game progress. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device.

2. How we use information

  • to provide core game features (sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, progress);
  • to keep the Game working correctly.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties, except Google as described above.

3. Third-party services

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game. Play Games data can be managed or deleted in your Google account settings or in the Google Play Games app. You can also contact us to request deletion.

6. Security

We rely on Google's infrastructure to protect data transmitted through Play Games Services.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
